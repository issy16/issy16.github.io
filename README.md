### What I'm building

**[Mindsea](https://issy16.github.io/mindsea/)**: a deep-sea themed mind map maker. Branch out ideas, link opposites, and sink deeper as you zoom in. Sign in with Google to sync your maps across devices; edits made offline upload when you reconnect. *Early preview. Try it and tell me what you think!*

**[System Design Lab](https://issy16.github.io/systemdesignlab/)**: learn system design by breaking a simulated concert ticket app, then fixing it. Push traffic, cause the failure, apply the fix, and see why it works. 23 interactive pages so far, in English, 繁體中文 and 简体中文. [Source](https://github.com/issy16/systemdesignlab)

**[Meowdoku Solver](https://github.com/issy16/meowdoku)**: solves Meowdoku puzzles with constraint satisfaction and backtracking, and reports whether a puzzle has no solution, exactly one or several. Screenshot import and step-by-step hints are next.
